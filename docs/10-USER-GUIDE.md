# Arise — User Guide

How to actually use the app, day to day. Arise is a fitness RPG: you level up a Hunter by
**training honestly**. There is no way to gain XP except by doing real work — completing your
programmed sets, adding weight/reps over time, and honoring recovery. That's the whole game.

---

## Install it (do this once)

Arise is a PWA — it installs like a real app, works offline, and lives on your home screen.

1. Open the deployed URL in your phone browser (Chrome/Safari).
2. **Add to Home Screen** (Share → Add to Home Screen on iOS; the install prompt or ⋮ menu on
   Android/Chrome).
3. Launch it from the icon (the violet crown). First load caches everything for offline use.

No account is required — everything is stored on your device. (Optional cloud sync is in
**Settings**; see *Account & sync* below.)

---

## The dashboard (your home screen)

- **ARISE** — the System wordmark; tap **[PROFILE]** / **[SYSTEM]** (top right) for your records
  and settings.
- **Status Window** — your Hunter standing in their aura, your **level**, **tier name**, and the
  **XP bar** to the next level.
- **Weekly Roster** — Mon–Sun, showing gym days (your aura colour), shift days, and rest. **Tap
  any day** (today or earlier) to train it — see *Training off-schedule* below.
- **Today's panel** — changes with the day:
  - **Gym day:** the gate to enter (e.g. *Crimson Forge*). Tap **ENTER GATE**.
  - **Shift day:** the **Mandatory Quest** — tap to confirm you endured the shift (+REC).
  - **Rest day:** the **Sanctuary** — recovery is judged at dawn (see *Rest days*).
- **Daily Quests** — 2–3 per day (see *Quests*).
- **Hunter Profile** — your four stats (STR/VIT/AGI/REC) and the **Training Log** heatmap of your
  streak.

## Doing a workout (clearing a Gate)

1. **Enter the gate** from the dashboard (or tap a roster day to pick any gate).
2. **Tap an exercise** to expand it — you'll see the muscle heatmap, form cues, and the set
   logger.
3. **Log each set:** the weight/reps are pre-filled from last time. Adjust with the ± steppers,
   then tap **LOG**. Aim for ≤3 taps per set.
   - **Grades** appear as you log vs the same set last session: ▲ beat · ▶ held · ▼ down · ◆ new.
   - **REST timer:** a bar appears at the top counting down that exercise's rest. It buzzes when
     you're good to go. Tap **×** to dismiss.
   - **LOAD / WARM-UP** (barbell lifts): under the sets you'll see the plate loadout per side for
     your weight, and a **WARM-UP** ramp (40/60/80%) you can expand.
   - **Fix a set:** tap a logged row to reopen and **SAVE** an amendment.
4. When every set of every exercise is done, **CLEAR GATE**.
5. The **Tally** itemizes your spoils (set XP, progression bonuses, your streak multiplier, any
   boss kills) and counts up your total. If you crossed a level, the **Ceremony** plays.

### Training off-schedule (shifts change)
Tap **any past or current day** on the roster → **choose a gate** (Push / Pull / Legs) → log it
for that date. Editing a past day saves quietly (no ceremony). Everything — XP, level, streak,
quests — recomputes automatically, because the app only ever replays your honest log.

## How leveling works (the honesty law)

XP comes only from real outcomes:
- **Sets** — each programmed set pays XP scaled by tonnage (junk volume is worthless; you can't
  farm reps).
- **Progression** — beating last session's tonnage and adding weight (vs your all-time max)
  pays bonuses. Sandbagging then "PRing" back doesn't.
- **Recovery** — confirmed shifts (+REC) and honored rest days (+REC).
- **Streak** — fully-honored training weeks multiply your gate XP (up to ×1.5).

100 levels across **10 tiers** (E-Rank → Absolute God of Shadows). Your **aura colour and the
whole UI** recolour as you climb. Your four stats — **STR / VIT / AGI / REC** — grow from the
kind of work you do.

### Bosses, relics, titles
- **Bosses** are weight milestones on your main lifts (every +10 kg compound / +5 kg accessory).
  Hold the weight for all programmed sets to "defeat" it — worth bonus XP.
- **Relics** are earned PRs and milestones; **Titles** are tier names you can equip in the
  profile. Both live in your profile.

## Quests, shifts, and rest

- **Daily Quests** (2–3/day): some **auto-claim** when you do the work (e.g. clear the gate, beat
  tonnage), some are **manual** (tap CLAIM, e.g. review form), and some are **intrinsic** (the
  shift/rest bonus itself — they don't double-pay).
- **Shift days:** tap **TRIAL ENDURED** on the Mandatory Quest to log the shift (+REC). Read the
  quest intel for work-day guidance.
- **Rest days (Sanctuary):** do nothing and recovery is honored automatically at dawn (+REC). If
  you train on a rest day, that day's rest bonus is forfeit — but the training XP stands.

## Your body & nutrition (Profile)

Open **[PROFILE]**. Beyond stats, records, relics, titles and the tier gallery:

- **Body Weight** — log it when a quest asks (or any time); it drives the chart and your nutrition
  targets.
- **Provisions (nutrition)** — pick a goal (**CUT / MAINTAIN / BULK**). Arise sets a **protein**
  target (~2 g/kg) and a **calorie** target from your bodyweight. Log today's protein + calories
  with the steppers; the bars fill toward target. *(Nutrition is tracking only — it doesn't give
  XP. Honest food, honest data.)*
- **Measurements** — pick a site (waist/chest/arm/thigh), set the cm, **LOG**. Each site gets its
  own trend sparkline.
- **System Records** — per lift: sessions, best set, **e1RM** (estimated 1-rep max), and a
  top-weight sparkline.
- **Training Log** — the streak heatmap: trained / honored-rest / shift / missed, by day.

## Account & sync (optional)

Everything works offline with no account. In **[SYSTEM]** (Settings) you can:
- **Link an account** (Google or email magic-link) to **sync across devices** — train offline on
  your phone and desktop and both converge.
- **Import history** from Shift + Lift — paste your old export or upload the file; it replays
  through the same honest XP rules (and earns the *Imported Soul* relic).

A small **sync dot** in the dashboard nav shows status (idle / syncing / error). Sync never
blocks you and never wipes your data on its own — only an explicit sign-out does.

## Tips
- The app is most honest if you log **as you train** — the rest timer and live grades are built
  for that.
- Don't chase the XP; chase the work. The XP follows.
- Character art fills in over time (each level gets its own portrait). Until a level's art exists,
  your aura silhouette stands in — nothing is missing, it just upgrades.
- Trouble seeing a change after an update? You're on a PWA — fully close and reopen the app, or
  accept the System's update prompt when it appears.
